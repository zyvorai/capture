Record it once. Share it clean.

  • Record what you need

    A display, one window or an area, with system audio and the microphone if you want them, a countdown and a hotkey to start and stop.

  • Idle time, handled

    Stretches where the pointer neither moves nor clicks play faster, or are cut. The action around them stays at normal speed.

  • A real timeline

    Zoom, caption, cut and slow-motion blocks sit on lanes under the video. Drag and resize them. Zoom blocks are generated from your clicks.

  • Webcam and clips

    Record your camera beside the screen and place it as a bubble in any corner. Join other clips with a crossfade, slide, push or wipe.

  • A smoother cursor

    Record without the system cursor, then draw a smoothed, enlarged one with a click ring, so a demo reads clearly.

  • Secrets hidden

    On-device text recognition finds emails, API keys, tokens and card numbers and pixelates them only while they are on screen. A helper, not a guarantee: check before you share.

  • A real photo editor

    Crop to a shape, straighten, adjust and filter; arrows, shapes, text and numbered steps; one click to hide secrets; copy text or read a QR code; cut out the subject; beautify with a background, shadow and window frame.

  • Polish and export

    Trim to the action, spotlight the pointer, frame the video, fade in and out, and export MP4 or GIF at 1080p, 720p or 480p. Pictures export as PNG, JPEG or HEIC.

  • No network

    No networking code and no account. Microphone and camera are its special entitlements, and each is asked only when you turn it on. Captions from speech use macOS's on-device recognizer.

Questions, answered.

What can it record?

A whole display, one window or an area, with optional system audio and microphone. Control-Option-Command-V starts and stops; Control-Option-Command-P takes a screenshot.

What does Smart edit do?

It speeds up or cuts idle time, zooms in on clicks, shows click rings and pixelates emails, API keys, tokens and card numbers. It uses pointer positions saved beside recordings made in the app.

Can it find everything sensitive?

No. It looks for common patterns in text it can read on screen. It can miss things and can flag harmless text, so check the result before you share.

Which permissions does it ask for?

Screen Recording, the first time you record or take a screenshot. Microphone and Camera only if you turn them on. Speech Recognition only when you ask for captions from speech.

Can it record my webcam?

Yes. It is saved beside the recording, and you place it as a bubble in a corner when you export. The preview bubble is not recorded.

Does it send anything over the network?

No. It contains no networking code. Speech captions use the on-device recognizer in macOS, which may download a language pack once. Check it yourself with codesign -d --entitlements - "/Applications/Zyvor Capture.app". It is not sandboxed.

Does it log what I type?

No. It notes where the pointer is and when you click, only during a recording, and saves that beside the video. You can delete that file.

Can it remove objects from photos?

Not in this release. It can cut out the subject, redact areas, and annotate.

Is there a trial?

Yes: a 30-day trial key from sales@zyvor.dev. Without a key everything still works.

What happens without a key, or when a trial ends?

Nothing is disabled. A status line shows where your licence stands.

Which macOS versions?

macOS 27 or later, Apple silicon.
